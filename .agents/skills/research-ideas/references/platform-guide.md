# Platform research guide

## Instagram

- **Formats:** Reels (9:16), carousel-to-Reel teasers
- **Viral patterns:** Before/after, POV, "day in the life", tutorial hooks
- **Hook window:** 1–3 seconds
- **Research queries:** `"[niche] instagram reels viral 2026"`, `"[competitor] instagram reels"`

## YouTube

- **Formats:** Shorts (9:16), long-form B-roll, intros
- **Viral patterns:** Challenge, reaction, "I tried X for 30 days"
- **Hook window:** 3–5 seconds for Shorts
- **Research queries:** `"[niche] youtube shorts viral"`, `"[topic] youtube trend"`

## TikTok

- **Formats:** Native vertical video, duet/stitch bait
- **Viral patterns:** Trending sounds (note: Magic Hour generates video; add audio in post), POV, skits
- **Hook window:** 1–2 seconds
- **Research queries:** `"[niche] tiktok viral format"`, `"tiktok [product category] content ideas"`

## X (Twitter)

- **Formats:** 16:9 or 1:1 clips, GIF-style loops
- **Viral patterns:** Hot takes, demos, meme reactions
- **Hook window:** Autoplay first frame must intrigue
- **Research queries:** `"[niche] twitter video viral"`, `"[competitor] x video posts"`

## Magic Hour alignment

Every idea must map to text-to-video:
- Describe the **visual scene** in the prompt (not editing instructions).
- Specify aspect ratio and duration per platform.
